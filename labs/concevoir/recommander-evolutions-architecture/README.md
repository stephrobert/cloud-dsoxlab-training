# Turn architecture findings into a prioritised action plan

This lab turns review findings into actions a team can verify and deliver.

## What you will be able to do

- Write recommendations with measurable criteria, expected effects, effort,
  cost and residual risk.
- Rank findings and schedule dependent work without exceeding team capacity.

## Evidence boundary

The risks and estimates are controlled exercise data. The lab evaluates the
quality and feasibility of the plan, not an actual provider environment.
