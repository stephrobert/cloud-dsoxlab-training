# Scenario: a review that has no decisions

The customer portal has been designed and exercised in a local capstone. Its
team has collected configuration, recovery, load-test, cost-model and
sovereignty evidence, but the review is still a collection of disconnected
notes.

A scenario says **where we are and what is wrong**, not the steps to take:
finding those is the learner's work.

## Mission

Use `review-evidence.json` to produce a concise `review.md` for the architecture
board and a machine-readable `review.json`. Score the six axes, cite the
evidence behind each score, rank the three highest risks, and record an ADR
decision and an accountable action for each. Include the evidence limits.

Skill targeted: `cloud.architecture.review`
